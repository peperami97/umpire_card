🏆 Herts Premier "110 Split" Deep Dive

In the Saracens Hertfordshire Premier Cricket League timed matches (mid-season weeks):

  • Match Duration: 110 overs total shared across both innings.
  • Team 1 Restriction: Capped at 58 overs maximum. If they bat 58 overs, innings is compulsorily closed.
  • Tactical Declaration Incentive: If Team 1 declares after 45 overs, Team 2 receives $110 - 45 = 65$ overs to chase!
  • The Last Hour: Must be called and signaled at the designated time (e.g. 5:30pm or 1 hour prior to scheduled close). A minimum of 20 overs must be bowled.
  • Batting Bonus Points (starts at 110): 110=1, 125=2, 140=3, 155=4, 170=5, 185=6, 200=7, 215=8, 230=9, 245=10 pts.
  • Bowling Bonus Points: 3 wkts=1, 5 wkts=2, 7 wkts=3, 9 wkts=4, 10 wkts=5 pts.

⏱️ Over Rates & Cut-Off Rules

Target timings and slow over-rate in-match sanctions:

  • T20 Target: 85 minutes per innings (~4.25 mins/over). If over 20 hasn't started by the cut-off time, the bowling team loses 1 boundary fielder (only 4 allowed outside circle).
  • T50 Target: 3 hours 30 mins (210 mins, ~14.3 to 17 overs/hour).
  • Allowances to record: DRS reviews, player injuries/treatment, lost balls, sightscreen adjustments, batter delays.

🖨️ How to Print & Prepare Your Cards

For best results on the field:

  • Cardstock: Print on 160gsm to 220gsm card (matte white or off-white) so ballpoint pen and pencil will not bleed through.
  • Duplex Setting: Select "Print on Both Sides" (Flip on Long Edge) for A4 portrait. Page 1 prints on the front (Innings 1 / Setup), Page 2 on the back (Innings 2 / Target / Result).
  • Pocket Wallet: Cards fit into standard umpire pocket wallets, notebook holders, or weather-resistant transparent PVC sleeves.
